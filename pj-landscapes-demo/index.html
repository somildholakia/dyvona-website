Before we get started.

Not sure what your garden needs? Send over a few details and Pat can discuss the next step with you.

Do you offer free, no-obligation quotes?

Yes. P&J Landscapes offers free, no-obligation quotes. Tell Pat what you would like done and where the property is based, and he can discuss the work with you.

Which areas does P&J Landscapes cover?

The business is based in Aylesbury and serves customers across Buckinghamshire, Oxfordshire and Hertfordshire. Share your postcode when you enquire and Pat can confirm whether your location is within the service area.

What landscaping and garden services do you provide?

Services include garden design, patios, flagging and paths, decorative stone and shingle driveways, fencing and borders, artificial lawns, turfing, rotavating, levelling, decking, raised sleeper beds, rockeries, tree and hedge care, garden clearances, weeding, shed installation and power washing.

Can you help with patios, paving and garden paths?

Yes. P&J Landscapes undertakes patios, flagging and garden paths, as well as decorative stone and shingle work. Describe the area and the finish you have in mind when requesting a quote.

Do you install artificial lawns and lay natural turf?

Artificial lawns and turfing are both listed services. Ground preparation such as rotavating and levelling can also be discussed as part of your garden project.

Can you replace fencing or improve garden boundaries?

Yes. Fencing and borders are part of the service range. Get in touch with details of the area, the type of work required and your location.

Do you carry out tree work and hedge trimming?

Tree services include planting, pruning, topping and felling or removal. Hedge services include care, trimming and removal. Share the type of work you need so Pat can discuss the next step with you.

Can you clear an overgrown garden or remove weeds?

Yes. Garden clearances and weeding are among the services offered. You can use the enquiry form to describe the size of the area and the work you would like considered.

Do you build decking, raised sleeper beds or rockeries?

Decking, raised sleeper beds and rockeries are included in the listed services. Send Pat a short description of your ideas to discuss what you have in mind.

Can you install a garden shed or power-wash outdoor areas?

Garden shed installation and power washing are both offered. General garden and property maintenance can also be discussed when you get in touch.

How much does landscaping or garden maintenance cost?

There is no single price that suits every garden. The work required and the details of your project need to be discussed before a quote can be prepared. Contact Pat for a free, no-obligation quote rather than relying on a generic online estimate.

What information should I include when I request a quote?

It helps to include your postcode, the service you are interested in and a short description of the garden or area involved. You can add any useful measurements or details you already have, but you do not need to know every technical detail before making an enquiry.

How do I contact Pat?

You can call 07918 831065, email patlandscaping@yahoo.com or use the enquiry form. The form prepares a message in WhatsApp for you to review; you choose whether to send it.
