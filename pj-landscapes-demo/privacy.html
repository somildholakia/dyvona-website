Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens when you use the P&J Landscapes website to prepare an enquiry. It is written for this website's current contact process.

Enquiry form and WhatsApp

The website enquiry form runs in your browser. When you choose “Continue to WhatsApp”, the website prepares a message using the details you entered and opens WhatsApp. The website itself does not send the message, store your form entries in a website database or submit the enquiry automatically. You can review, edit or close the message before deciding whether to send it.

If you choose to send the message, WhatsApp receives and processes it under its own terms and privacy policy. Read the WhatsApp Privacy Policy for information about its handling of messages and account data.

Calling or emailing

If you call 07918 831065 or email patlandscaping@yahoo.com, the information you provide is handled through your phone or email service and by P&J Landscapes to respond to your enquiry.

Website hosting and external resources

This website is hosted by Render. It also loads typefaces from Google Fonts and project photographs from Wix's image delivery service. When your browser requests these resources, those providers may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details. Please refer to the relevant providers' privacy information for their practices.

Questions about privacy

For a question about an enquiry you have sent to P&J Landscapes, contact Pat by email at patlandscaping@yahoo.com or by phone on 07918 831065.

This notice describes the current website behaviour. P&J Landscapes should review and approve it before the website is moved to its permanent business domain or any additional analytics, advertising, booking or form services are introduced.
